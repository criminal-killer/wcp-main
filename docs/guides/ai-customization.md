## How the AI Works

Chatevo's AI acts as your 24/7 sales assistant on WhatsApp. When a customer messages your store, the AI:

1. Greets them with your custom greeting
2. Helps them browse products
3. Answers questions
4. Guides them through checkout
5. Sends order confirmations

---

## Customizing Your Greeting

The greeting is the first message customers see. Make it:

1. Go to **Settings** → **AI & Intelligence**
2. Find **System Prompt** (first line = greeting)
3. Write a friendly, professional greeting

### Example Greetings

**Casual/Friendly:**
```
Hey! Welcome to Sarah's Fashion 👋 We're here to help you find the perfect outfit!
```

**Professional:**
```
Good day! Thank you for reaching out to Luxe Electronics. How can I assist you today?
```

**With Offer:**
```
Hi there! 👋 Welcome to TechZone Kenya. We have amazing deals this week! What are you looking for?
```

## Custom Instructions

Add special instructions in the System Prompt field:

- "Always mention our free shipping on orders over $50"
- "Recommend complementary products after each purchase"
- "Offer gift wrapping for $5 extra"

---

## AI Provider Settings

### Default (Chatevo AI - Free)
Uses Groq's Llama model. No setup needed.

### Custom AI (Pro/Elite only)
Connect your own API for more control:

- **OpenAI** (GPT-4)
- **Google Gemini**
- **Anthropic Claude**

---

## Testing Your AI

1. Go to **Settings** → **AI & Intelligence**
2. Click **Test Chatbot**
3. Send a test message
4. See how the AI responds

---

## Tips for Better AI Responses

1. **Be specific** in custom instructions
2. **Mention your unique selling points**
3. **Set clear boundaries** ("We don't offer refunds on digital products")
4. **Add FAQs** to your product descriptions

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| AI not responding | Check your AI settings are saved |
| Wrong responses | Update system prompt with clearer instructions |
| Too generic | Add product-specific info to system prompt |
