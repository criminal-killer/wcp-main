## Step 1: Sign Up

1. Go to [chatevo-app.vercel.app](https://chatevo-app.vercel.app)
2. Click **Get Started** or **Sign Up**
3. You can sign up with:
   - **Email/Password**
   - **Google Account**
   - **Phone Number**

## Step 2: Create Your Store

After signing up, you'll be asked to:
1. **Store Name** — e.g., "Sarah's Fashion"
2. **Country** — Select your country (affects currency)
3. **Business Type** — Choose the category that best fits

## Step 3: Choose a Plan

| Plan | Price | Best For |
|------|-------|----------|
| Starter | $29/mo | New merchants (100 products) |
| Pro | $59/mo | Growing stores (500 products) |
| Elite | $99/mo | High-volume sellers (unlimited) |

**All plans include 7-day free trial.**

## Step 4: Set Up Your Dashboard

Once signed up, you'll see the merchant dashboard. From here you can:
- Connect WhatsApp
- Add products
- Set up payments
- Customize your AI assistant
