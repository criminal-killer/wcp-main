## 🎥 Video 3: Connecting Your WhatsApp AI Assistant
* **Duration:** ~2:30 mins

* **AI Voice Tone:** [Empowering, Authoritative, Tech-Enlightening, Smooth]
* **Voice Style:** Calm, precise, reassuring (reduces technical anxiety).

| Time | On-Screen Action (Visuals) | Voiceover Script (Audio) | Emotional Cue (Pacing) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:20** | Cursor navigates to **Settings** -> **WhatsApp** in Chatevo. A box displays "Callback URL" and "Verify Token". Click "Verify and Save". | "First, head to your **Meta Developers Console**. In your WhatsApp Configuration, click Edit. | **0:50 - 1:20** | Show Meta Business Settings -> System Users page.
